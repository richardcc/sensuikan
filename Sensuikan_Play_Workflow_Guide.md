### TABLERO TÁCTICO CHART K1: CAPITÁN Y TRIPULACIÓN
El tablero **Chart K1** gestiona la jerarquía, condecoraciones y habilidades adquiridas:

1. **Capitán:**
   - **Rango:** Comienza como *Lt Cdr* (Teniente Comandante / 少佐). Ascenso a *Commander* (Comandante / 中佐) y *Captain* (Capitán de Navío / 大佐) por tonelaje y supervivencia.
   - **Habilidades Iniciales/Adquiribles:**
     - *Shibumi* (Serenidad estoica ante cargas de profundidad: -1 a la tirada de detección o daño de escolta).
     - *Leadership* (Liderazgo: +1 a las tiradas de reparación del Ingeniero y temple de la tripulación).
     - *Torpedo Gunnery* (Tiro de Torpedos: +1 a los impactos con torpedos Tipo 95 / Tipo 93).
   - **Medallas y Condecoraciones:**
     - *Order of the Sacred Treasure* (Orden del Tesoro Sagrado - Inicial).
     - *Order of the Golden Kite (5th & 4th Class)* (Orden del Milano Dorado - por patrullas sobresalientes y buques de guerra hundidos).
     - *Order of the Rising Sun* (Orden del Sol Naciente - por hazañas excepcionales >60,000 toneladas).
     - *Wound Badge* (Insignia de Herido en Combate).
     - *GEA War Medal* (Medalla de la Gran Guerra de Asia Oriental).
     - *Sub Badge* (Insignia de Guerra Submarina IJN).

2. **Oficiales y Especialistas:**
   - **XO (Segundo Oficial):** *Vigilant* (Vigilancia nocturna, bono de avistamiento pre-combate), *Deck Gun Expert* (Artillero de cubierta), *AA Gun Expert* (Antiaéreo).
   - **Ingeniero (Engineer):** *Expert*, *Mechanical Genius* (Genio mecánico: +1 a reparaciones), *Keen Judgement* (Juicio certero en control de averías).
   - **Navegante (Navigator):** *Expert* (Permite evitar contactos aéreos y recalcular rutas de escape).
   - **Médico (Medic):** *Expert* (Cura heridas de oficiales y previene bajas mortales).
   - **Dotación Aérea (Aichi E13A / E14Y Glen / M6A Seiran):** *Weapons Expert*, *Aircraft Maintenance*, *A.C.M.* (Maniobras de combate), *Aerial Navigation*.
   - **Dotación Mini-Submarinos (Ko-hyoteki / Kaiten):** *Midget Torpedo Gunnery*, *Boat Handling*, *Superior Comms*.

## 🇬🇧 ENGLISH: PLAY WORKFLOW & OPERATIONAL RULES GUIDE

This guide provides the complete 5-phase play sequence matching the official flow chart (`manual-flujo-juego-sensuikan.pdf`), Gregory M. Smith's solitaire campaign rules, and the tactical counter management on **Chart K1 Captain & Crew Skill Status** (as featured on Zilla Blitz Submarine School).

### Phase 0: Submarine & Commander Selection (Rule 4.0 & Chart H2)
1. **Submarine Selection:** Choose an Imperial Japanese Navy fleet submarine class historically available for the campaign scenario (Kaidai KD.3-KD.7, Junsen J.1-J.3, Type A, Type B, Type C, Kirai-sen, or Sen-Toku I-400).
2. **Hull Identifier:** Assign vessel number (e.g. *I-19*, *I-58*, *I-21*, *I-168*).
3. **Commander Name (Chart H2):** Determine Captain surname and given name by rolling 1d6 + 1d6 on Chart H2 (or choose a historical IJN ace).
4. **Starting Rank & Base:** Commission the vessel with starting rank (Lt Cdr or Commander) and home naval base (Kure, Yokosuka, Truk, Penang).
5. **Proceed to Training:** Log boat specs in the Rogu-Bukku and dispatch officers and crew to Naval Training Schools.

### Training Schools Phase (Rule 4.8 & Charts F1, F2, F3)
Prior to departing on the first war patrol, officers and crew undergo pre-deployment training in home waters:

#### 1. Chart F1: Submarine School (Kure)
- **Captain - Submarine Command Course (*Koshu gakusei*):**
  - *Torpedo Training:* Roll 3 times on Chart [B1] using the long range column (hit on 10+ on 2d6). If at least 2 are hits, place the **"Torpedo Gunnery"** skill marker on Chart [K1]. The player may re-roll a "miss" on Chart [B1] once per patrol by using this skill.
- **Executive Officer (XO) - Submarine Officers Course (*Otsushu gakusei*):**
  - *Deck Gun Training:* Roll 3 times on Chart [B1] using the long range column. If at least 2 are hits, place the **"Deck Gun Expert"** skill marker on Chart [K1]. Deck Gun "to hit" rolls will be at -1 DRM. This skill is lost if the XO leaves the boat.
- **Engineer - Submarine Engineering Course (*Kikan gakusei*):**
  - *Judgment Training:* Starting with 3 hull damage, use the rules to Exceed Test Depth 3 times, increasing notional hull damage each time before rolling again (damage 3, then 4, then 5; implosion occurs if roll + damage >= 13). If none of the results are "implosion", the Engineer gains the **"Keen Judgment"** skill marker on Chart [K1]. Allows the player not to take hull damage the first time they exceed test depth on each patrol (lost if Engineer leaves the boat).
- **Captain's Spare Time Activities (Choose 1 of 3):**
  1. *Martial Arts Training:* The player develops mind and body. Gain the **"Shibumi"** (effortless perfection) marker on Chart [K1]. May be used once per patrol to automatically make a die or dice roll check.
  2. *Extra Study Time:* Graduating with honors at top of class. Starts game with a free **"Submarine Upgrade"** marker and gets **-2 to first Promotion attempt**.
  3. *Gosei (Meditation):* Looking inward. Start game with a **"Maneki Neko"** (good luck charm) marker.
- **Graduation:** Congratulations! You and your crew have graduated from Submarine School at Kure. Place the **Sub Badge** on your uniform on Chart [K1] and prepare to patrol.

#### 2. Chart F2: Midget Submarine School (Type C boats carrying Ko-Hyoteki)
- **Submarine Command Course - Torpedo Training:** Roll 3 times on Chart [B1] long range column. If 2+ hits, gain **"Midget Torpedo Gunnery"** on Chart [K1] (re-roll 1 miss on [B1] per patrol).
- **Submarine Officers Course - Communications:** Roll twice on Sub Recovery Chart [M7]. If neither fails, gain +1 drm for the rest of the game and place **"Superior Comms"** marker on Chart [K1].
- **Submarine Engineering Course - Boat Handling:** Roll 3 times on Anti-Submarine Net Chart [M5]. If none result in "2-4", a result of "4" on [M5] becomes "Through Net" for the rest of the game, place **"Boat Handling"** on Chart [K1].

#### 3. Chart F3: Kasumigaura Naval Air School (Submarines with Aircraft - Choose exactly 2 options)
- **Weapons Expert:** Bombing rolls are at +1. Rear machine-gun "to-hit" rolls are at -1. Place **"Weapons Expert"** on Chart [K1].
- **Flawless Maintenance:** Re-roll any result from Mishap/Malfunction Chart once per mission. Place **"Aircraft Maintenance"** on Chart [K1].
- **Air Combat Maneuvering (ACM):** The first damage result per combat (only) is reduced by 1 point. Place **"ACM"** on Chart [K1].
- **Aerial Navigation:** Recovery roll with Submarine after a flight gets a +1 drm. Place **"Aerial Navigation"** skill marker on Chart [K1].

### Phase 1: Departure & Assignment
1. **Timeframe Selection:** Patrol date advances month-by-month from Dec 1941 to Jul 1945 (46 possible months).
2. **Naval Base:** Kure / Yokosuka, Truk Lagoon, Penang / Singapore, or Rabaul.
3. **Patrol Assignment Table [A1]:** Roll 2d6 to determine operational patrol box (e.g., Pearl Harbor, Coral Sea, Indian Ocean, Solomons, Mariana Islands).
4. **Special Directives & Major Historical Battles:** Check for Midway (06/42), Guadalcanal (08/42), Philippine Sea (06/44), Leyte Gulf (10/44).

### Phase 2: Navigation & Patrol Track
1. **Movement:** Advance counter box by box along your assigned submarine operational track.
2. **Transit Boxes:** Safe transit until August 1942 (only natural 12 triggers Random Event [B11]). From 08/42 onwards, regular enemy encounter rolls apply.
3. **Encounter Roll (Table [A3] - 2d6):**
   - **2-4:** Enemy Aircraft Encounter (Escort detection or Air Attack Table [B8]).
   - **5:** No Contact (*Radar Check:* If radar is installed, roll again immediately!).
   - **6-11:** Naval Encounter (Proceed to Phase 3 Tactical Combat).
   - **12:** Random Historical Event Table [B11].

### Phase 3: Tactical Combat
1. **Target Identification:** Roll on Target Tables [T1]-[T5] to determine ship type, class, tonnage, and escort screen.
2. **Environmental Conditions:** Day / Night surface / Night submerged.
3. **Attack Range Selection:**
   - *Long Range* (Segura, mayor dispersión).
   - *Medium Range* (Equilibrada).
   - *Close Range* (Máxima letalidad; requires pre-attack infiltration check 2d6 <= 9 on [B4]; if detected, attack is aborted and escorts attack immediately).
4. **Torpedo Salvo Execution:**
   - Launch forward or aft tubes (Type 95 Oxygen torpedoes / Type 93 / Type 89).
   - Roll 2d6 on Table [B2] modifying for range, night optics, and *Torpedo Gunnery* skill.
   - Check Torpedo Duds.
   - Roll Damage Points on Table [H1]. If damage >= hull rating, Target is **SUNK**!
5. **Escort Counter-Attack & Depth Charges (Table [B5]):**
   - Escort aggressiveness roll + sonar detection.
   - Depth charges or Hedgehog mortars (from 1943).
   - Submarine damage resolved on Table [B6] (System damage) and Table [B7] (Hull flooding).

### Phase 4: Damage Control & Repair
1. **Damage Control:** Engineer tests to restore INOP systems to operational status.
2. **Flooding Pumping:** Pump out 1 partial flooding level per turn.
3. **Abort Mission Determination:** Submarine must abort back to base if severe hull integrity is breached, dive planes are disabled, or all torpedoes are expended.

### Phase 5: Return to Base, Refit & Promotions
1. **Patrol Logging:** Record sunk tonnage and targets into the 46-month **Sensuikan Rogu Bukku**.
2. **Refit Calculation ('R' Months in Port):**
   - 1 base month of maintenance.
   - +1 month if 3+ systems remain inoperative (INOP).
   - +1 month per 3 hull damage boxes sustained (or fraction thereof).
3. **Port Bombing Check:** Roll for enemy air raids while docked (Rule 8.5.11.4).
4. **Crew Recovery & Skills:** Wounded crew heal. Every 3 successful completed patrols award 1 new skill marker on **Chart K1**.

## 🏫 TABLAS OFICIALES DE ADIESTRAMIENTO (CHARTS F1, F2, F3)

### CHART F1: SUBMARINE SCHOOL (ESCUELA DE SUBMARINOS EN KURE)
*Ubicación: Kure Naval District. Requisito de Regla 4.8 antes de la primera patrulla.*

#### 1. Captain - Submarine Command Course (*Koshu gakusei*)
- **Curriculum:** *Tactics, Strategy, Torpedo Attacks, Ship Handling, Navigation, Construction, and Engineering.*
- **Torpedo Training:** Tirar 3 veces en la columna de Largo Alcance (Long Range) de la Tabla [B1] (impacto con 10+ en 2d6). Si al menos 2 son impactos, colocar la ficha de habilidad **Torpedo Gunnery** en Chart [K1]. El jugador puede repetir una tirada fallida de torpedo en Chart [B1] una vez por patrulla.

#### 2. Executive Officer (XO) - Submarine Officers Course (*Otsushu gakusei*)
- **Curriculum:** *Tactics, Submarine Operations, Communications, Diving, Construction, and Engineering.*
- **Deck Gun Training:** Tirar 3 veces en la columna de Largo Alcance de la Tabla [B1] (impacto con 10+ en 2d6). Si al menos 2 son impactos, colocar la ficha **Deck Gun Expert** en Chart [K1]. Las tiradas de impacto con cañón de cubierta reciben un modificador de -1 DRM continuo. La habilidad se pierde si el Segundo Comandante abandona el buque.

#### 3. Engineer - Submarine Engineering Course (*Kikan gakusei*)
- **Curriculum:** *Engineering, Construction, Trimming and Tactics.*
- **Judgment Training:** Comenzando con 3 daños de casco, aplicar las reglas para Exceder Profundidad de Test 3 veces consecutivas, aumentando el daño nocional antes de cada tirada (+3 daño, +4 daño, +5 daño). Si ninguno de los resultados es "implosion" (2d6 + daño >= 13), el Maquinista obtiene la ficha **Keen Judgment** en Chart [K1]. Permite ignorar el daño de casco la primera vez que se exceda la profundidad de test en cada patrulla. Se pierde si el Maquinista abandona el buque (ver Regla 8.2.7).

#### 4. Spare Time Activities (Actividades en Tiempo Libre del Capitán - Elegir 1 de 3)
1. **Martial Arts Training (Artes Marciales):** Desarrolla cuerpo y mente. Gana la ficha **"Shibumi"** (perfección sin esfuerzo) en Chart [K1]. Puede usarse una vez por patrulla para superar automáticamente cualquier tirada de un dado o dados.
2. **Extra Study Time (Horas Extra de Estudio):** El capitán dedica su tiempo libre al estudio técnico, graduándose con honores. Comienza con una ficha gratis de **"Submarine Upgrade"** y recibe un **-2 DRM en su primer intento de Promoción**.
3. **Gosei (Meditación Interior):** Serenidad espiritual y concentración. Comienza la partida con la ficha amuleto **"Maneki Neko"** (amuleto de la buena suerte).

#### 5. Graduation (Graduación)
- **Congratulations!** ¡Enhorabuena! Usted y su dotación se han graduado en la Escuela de Submarinos de Kure. Colocar el distintivo **Sub Badge** en su uniforme en Chart [K1] y prepararse para zarpar.

### CHART F2: MIDGET SUBMARINE SCHOOL (ESCUELA DE SUBMARINOS ENANOS)
*Aplica a dotaciones de submarinos oceánicos portadores de minisubmarinos Tipo A Ko-Hyoteki (ej. Submarinos Tipo C).*

#### 1. Submarine Command Course (*Koshu gakusei*)
- **Curriculum:** *Tactics, Strategy, Torpedo Attacks, Ship Handling, Navigation, Construction, and Engineering.*
- **Torpedo Training:** Tirar 3 veces en Chart [B1] columna de Largo Alcance. Si hay 2+ impactos, colocar la ficha **Midget Torpedo Gunnery** en Chart [K1]. Permite repetir un fallo en Chart [B1] una vez por patrulla con el minisubmarino.

#### 2. Submarine Officers Course (*Otsushu gakusei*)
- **Curriculum:** *Tactics, Submarine Operations, Communications, Diving, Construction, and Engineering.*
- **Communications:** Tirar 2 veces en la Tabla de Recuperación del Submarino [M7]. Si ninguna resulta en fallo, la tabla tendrá un modificador de +1 drm continuo y se coloca la ficha **Superior Comms** en Chart [K1].

#### 3. Submarine Engineering Course (*Kikan gakusei*)
- **Curriculum:** *Engineering, Construction, Trimming and Tactics.*
- **Boat Handling:** Tirar 3 veces en la Tabla de Redes Antisubmarinas [M5]. Si ninguna tirada resulta en "2-4", cualquier tirada de "4" en [M5] se convierte en "A través de la Red" (*Through Net*) para el resto de la campaña. Colocar la ficha **Boat Handling** en Chart [K1].

### CHART F3: KASUMIGAURA NAVAL AIR SCHOOL (ESCUELA NAVAL AÉREA)
*Aplica a dotaciones aéreas de submarinos porta-hidroaviones (Tipos J.2, J.3, A1, A2, AM, B1, B2, B3 y Sen-Toku I-400).*
*Instrucciones: Elegir exactamente 2 de las 4 opciones disponibles:*

1. **Weapons Expert:** Habilidad natural con armamento balístico. Las tiradas de bombardeo aéreo reciben un modificador de **+1**. Las tiradas para impactar de la ametralladora trasera reciben **-1**. Colocar ficha **"Weapons Expert"** en Chart [K1].
2. **Flawless Maintenance:** Pericia sobresaliente en mecánica aeronáutica e inspección de aeronaves. Permite repetir cualquier resultado de la tabla de averías (*Mishap/Malfunction Chart*) una vez por misión de vuelo. Colocar ficha **"Aircraft Maintenance"** en Chart [K1].
3. **Air Combat Maneuvering (ACM):** Maniobras evasivas defensivas avanzadas. El primer resultado de daño sufrido por el hidroavión en combate aéreo se reduce en **1 punto**. Colocar ficha **"ACM"** en Chart [K1].
4. **Aerial Navigation:** Navegación oceánica de precisión. La tirada de recuperación con el submarino tras el vuelo recibe un modificador de **+1 drm**. Colocar ficha **"Aerial Navigation"** en Chart [K1].
